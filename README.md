## What I do

### 🏥 Bilun: the Matrix wants your clinic underbilled

Turkish clinics that bill the state insurer (SGK) lose real money every month.
Because the reimbursement rulebook (the SUT) is thousands of paragraphs long and nobody has time to read it.

**Bilun** is an executable **rules engine** that audits MEDULA invoices against the SUT: it finds the revenue clinics
forgot to bill and catches errors before the invoice ever reaches MEDULA.

### 📈 BIST quant: Everyone has a feeling about the market. I have a backtest.

[`bist-quant`](https://github.com/Safa675/bist-quant) is a factor research library for Borsa İstanbul.
Systematic edge first, opinions later.

I quadrupled the turkish market in raw returns while keeping a 2.9 sharpe. I have a regime filter detects the regime of the market. And i am applying the strats according to the existing market regime. I have a portfolio engine to run the tests with the exact setup and sizing i want. And signals. Basically the recipebook for equity selection to defeat markets.
